How we work

Prepared before it leaves the bench

Used compute is only a bargain if someone has already done the unglamorous work. That is the whole business.

01

Intake

Physical inspection, cooler and pad condition, VBIOS, power delivery, and a written note on prior use when we know it.

02

Load-test

Sustained workload on a test bench. We keep thermal and error logs. Cards that throttle oddly or throw ECC noise do not ship.

03

Grade

Condition described in plain language — cosmetic marks, cooler type, warranty start. You buy a known card, not a photograph.

04

Pack and clear

Foam, anti-static, commercial invoice, packing list, and an HS classification that matches the box. Then a tracking number.

After the sale

Warranty, freight, and paper
